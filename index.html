About RSBSA

What is RSBSA Registration?

The Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) is the official registry that makes a farmer a legitimate, recognized beneficiary of government agricultural programs, subsidies, insurance, and assistance.

  • ✓ 2×2 photo (recent)
  • ✓ Filled-out RSBSA Enrolment Form
  • ✓ Government-issued ID (PhilSys, Voter's, Driver's License, etc.)
  • ✓ Proof of land ownership: Tax Declaration, Land Title, or Barangay Certification of farm location
  • ✓ Geotag verification of farm by DA personnel
